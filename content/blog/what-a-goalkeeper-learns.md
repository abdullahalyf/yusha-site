I play in goal. It is a lonely position, and I like that. While the rest of the team runs around, the keeper stands still and watches the whole pitch.

## You see everything

From the goal line you can see where every player is. Good keepers talk a lot, because they are the only ones who can see the whole picture. I try to do that: call out the marker, tell the defenders to step up, keep everyone awake.

## Mistakes are loud

When a striker misses, the game goes on. When the keeper misses, everybody remembers. At first that scared me. Now I think of it as practice for staying calm. The next ball comes whether I am upset or not, so the only useful thing is to get ready for it.

## It helps with studying

Before an exam I think about the same things: stay calm, get set early, and forget the last mistake. It is a small habit, and I picked it up from a goal.
