I play video games, mostly with friends. I can play alone, but it is never as good.

## Teamwork you can feel

A team game shows you quickly who listens and who does not. If everyone talks and shares the plan, you win more. That is true on a football pitch too, and I like seeing the same idea in a different place.

## Limits

Games are fun, and they can eat a whole evening. I try to keep them for after study and tutoring, not instead of them. A game is a reward, not a plan.

## Games and code

Playing games made me curious about how they are built. I am not there yet, but it is one of the reasons I want to get better at programming.
