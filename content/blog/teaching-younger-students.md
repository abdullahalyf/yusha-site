I tutor students from Play to Class 10 in maths, science, English and ICT, online and at home. I started to earn something while studying, and I stayed because I enjoy it.

## You find out what you do not know

A child asks "why?" and you realise you only knew the rule, not the reason. Then you have to learn the reason. Teaching is the fastest way I know to check your own understanding.

## Patience

Some ideas take three or four tries. I try to find a new example each time, instead of repeating the same one more slowly.

## It helps my own course

Explaining an idea simply is the same skill I need when I write a program or answer an exam question. I think every student should teach something once.
