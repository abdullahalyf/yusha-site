Football is the sport I play most seriously, but cricket is the one that brings everybody together. Anyone can join, and nobody minds if the pitch is a bit rough.

## A game for a group

Cricket needs a lot of people and a lot of patience. That makes it a good excuse to spend an afternoon with friends. The score matters less than the talking between overs.

## Small rules we made up

Every group of friends ends up with its own rules: one tip and out, a boundary at the wall, the last over for whoever is losing. I like that the game bends to fit whoever is playing.

## What I take from it

Cricket is slow, so it teaches patience. I think that carries over to programming, where the answer often comes after a long wait and a lot of small tries.
